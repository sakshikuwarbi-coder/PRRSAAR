Policy Regarding Receipt of Pre-funded Instruments

In Compliance with SEBI Circular CIR/MIRSD/03/2011

In accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MIRSD/03/2011 dated June 9, 2011, stock brokers are required to maintain an audit trail for the source of funds received through pre-funded instruments like Demand Drafts (DD), Banker's Cheques, and Pay Orders.

GENERAL RULE: Prrsaar discourages the acceptance of cash and pre-funded instruments. Clients are advised to route all payments electronically via NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, or Account Payee Cheques drawn on their registered bank accounts.

1. Conditions for Acceptance of Pre-funded Instruments

If a client tenders a pre-funded instrument under unavoidable circumstances, credit shall be granted to the client ledger only when accompanied by suitable certification proving that the funds originated from the client's verified bank account:

  • A Certificate from the issuing bank verifying the name of the accountholder and bank account number from which the instrument was purchased by debiting the account.
  • Copy of the bank passbook or statement reflecting the debit entry for the creation of the Demand Draft/Pay Order, duly attested by bank officials.
  • A copy of the bank's requisition slip/challan bearing the seal, branch code, and signature of the issuing bank officer with applicant details clearly identifying the client.

2. Limit on Cash Purchase of Pre-funded Instruments

Under no circumstances shall any pre-funded instrument purchased against cash for an amount of ₹50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand Only) or more be accepted by Prrsaar. Cash deposits into Prrsaar's bank accounts are strictly prohibited.

3. Treatment of Unverified Instruments

If a pre-funded instrument is received without the mandatory bank certificate or supporting debit statement:

  • The instrument will not be credited to the client's ledger.
  • No exposure, margin, or trading limit shall be permitted against such unverified receipts.
  • The funds shall be returned to the issuing bank or refunded to the client's verified bank account after proper validation.

4. Maintenance of Audit Trail and Records

The Accounts Department maintains a centralized Pre-funded Instrument Register documenting the instrument number, issuing bank branch, client UCC, payment mode, and copies of accompanying bank confirmations for verification by internal and Exchange auditors.

PRRSAAR FAQs — Demat Account, Brokerage & Trading

Quick answers on free Demat & Trading account opening, zero brokerage equity delivery, flat ₹20 intraday & F&O charges, direct mutual funds, IPO, CDSL safety and margin calculator.

Getting Started & Safety

How do I open a Demat and Trading account with PRRSAAR?
PRRSAAR offers 100% paperless online Demat and Trading account opening with e-KYC. Keep your PAN card, Aadhaar linked to your mobile number, and bank account or debit card details ready. The process takes just 5–10 minutes and there is no account opening fee.
Is PRRSAAR a SEBI registered stock broker?
Yes. PRRSAAR (Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited) is registered with SEBI (INZ000027432) and is an approved member of NSE, BSE, MCX and NCDEX, along with CDSL depository services (DP IN-DP-563-2021). Brokerage will never exceed the SEBI prescribed limit.
Are my shares and investments safe with PRRSAAR?
Yes. Your shares, mutual funds and bonds are held electronically in your own CDSL Demat account, never mixed with broker funds. You receive transaction alerts directly from CDSL on your registered mobile and email for every debit or credit.
How much does a PRRSAAR account cost? (Account opening and AMC charges)
Account opening for Trading and Demat is ₹0 (free). The annual maintenance charge (AMC) for Demat account maintenance is ₹299 per year, with no hidden platform fees.
Which trading platforms and tools does PRRSAAR offer?
PRRSAAR offers the PRRSAARplus web trading terminal, a mobile trading app for Android and iOS, and a high-speed charting suite powered by TradingView with real-time market data, option chain analysis and price alerts. Trade stocks, F&O, currency, commodity and mutual funds from one app.

Trading, Charges & Support

What are the brokerage charges for equity delivery, intraday and F&O trading?
Equity delivery trades carry 0% brokerage. Intraday and derivatives (Futures and Options) trades are charged a flat ₹20 per executed order, irrespective of trade size. Statutory charges such as STT/CTT, exchange transaction charges, GST and SEBI turnover fees apply as per exchange norms with no hidden charges.
Can I invest in direct mutual funds and apply for IPOs for free?
Yes. You can invest in direct mutual funds through SIP or lump sum and apply to new IPOs using UPI AutoPay from a single PRRSAAR account with zero commission and no extra charges.
What margin or leverage is available for intraday trading?
PRRSAAR offers up to 5x leverage on intraday equity trading under the SEBI Margin Allocation System (MAS) framework, plus day trading margins and span plus exposure margins for F&O. The exact margin required can be checked with our free online margin calculator before placing orders.
How can I calculate brokerage or margin before placing a trade?
Use PRRSAAR's free brokerage calculator and margin calculator to estimate total charges including brokerage, STT, exchange transaction charges and GST, and to check the margin required for equity delivery, intraday and options trades.
What should I do if I have a complaint or grievance against PRRSAAR?
Email grievance@prrsaar.com or dpgrievances@prrsaar.com, or call 91-11-45350000 on trading days. Unresolved complaints can be escalated to the SEBI SCORES portal and the SMART ODR portal for online dispute resolution.

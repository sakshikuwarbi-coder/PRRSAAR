FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS

Our Core Product Offerings

Institutional-grade trading segments and wealth creation tools under one unified Demat account.

01

Equity

Trade cash market equities on NSE and BSE using PRRSAARplus and high-speed mobile terminals. Experience fast order execution, real-time market depth quotes, and advanced technical charting. Benefit from delivery holding with complete zero commission and competitive intraday exposure.

02

Derivatives (F&O)

Hedge market risks and trade high-liquidity stock and index future and option contracts. Access live option chain analytics, Greeks, open interest build-up, and multi-leg order types. Enjoy optimized intraday margins and transparent flat ₹20 brokerage across all contracts.

03

Mutual Funds

Build disciplined long-term wealth through handpicked direct equity, debt, and hybrid mutual fund baskets. Automate monthly SIPs or invest lumpsum in top AMCs without paying any distributor commissions. Track fund performance with automated portfolio rebalancing alerts and zero transaction fees.

04

Initial Public Offerings (IPO)

Apply for Mainboard and SME initial public offerings with paperless ease in under a minute. Seamless UPI auto-mandate blocking ensures funds stay in your bank until the day of allotment. Get live subscription numbers, grey market insights, and automated allotment status alerts.

05

Commodities

Trade Bullion (Gold & Silver), Crude Oil, Base Metals, and Agri-contracts directly on MCX and NCDEX. Access extended market hours up to 11:55 PM for flexible evening hedging and dynamic volatility management. Supported by daily market research reports, automated margin calculators, and dedicated trading desks.

06

Currency

Hedge foreign exchange volatility across major currency pairs including USDINR, EURINR, and GBPINR. Exchange-cleared settlement ensures zero counterparty default risks with low upfront capital requirements. Ideal for import-export businesses, corporate hedgers, and algorithmic currency traders.

07

Depository Participant Services

Prrsaar is registered as Depository Participant with CDSL. Prrsaar offers services related to demat accounts to our clients like demat, Transfer, Pledging, Freezing and Remat of securities.

08

GOVERNMENT BONDS

A government bond is a debt instrument issued by the Central and State Governments of India. Guaranteed periodic interest returns with 100% principal safety backed by sovereign guarantees.

  • Dematerialisation: ₹100 per certificate + ₹50 per courier charges

  • Net Banking Gateway: ₹7 + GST

  • Holding Statement (Digital): FREE* — Current & previous one year
  • Holding Statement (Hard Copy): ₹50 + GST one financial year
  • Auction Charges: On Actual
  • Call & Trade Charges: ₹49 + GST per order

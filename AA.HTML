WHO WE ARE

Company Overview

Empowering traders and investors with reliable execution, transparent pricing, and comprehensive market access.

Built on Experience. Driven by Innovation.

Founded in 2004 by a team of visionary entrepreneurs with vast expertise in Banking & Finance, PRRSAAR is a one-stop investment solutions provider headquartered in New Delhi, with branch offices across the country. Our team brings 40+ years of combined experience in the financial markets, and our ever-increasing base of over a thousand clients trusts us across equity, commodities, currency derivatives and debt markets.

Operating as an official registered stock broker and CDSL Depository Participant (DP), we offer a unified account ecosystem where your demat holdings, trading balances, and mutual fund portfolios stay interconnected securely.
